Tap Scan hardware. Your phone will ask permission for motion sensors, NFC, camera, microphone and location. Deny any you like and the rest still works. Nothing is recorded, saved or sent anywhere.

    Why is there no °C temperature?

    Android and iOS do not give websites access to temperature sensors, even with permission. No such permission exists for browsers. Only native apps can read them.

    So ThermoCheck measures what actually makes games lag: thermal throttling. When the phone gets hot, the system slows the CPU down. ThermoCheck runs a tiny CPU test every few seconds and compares it with your cool-phone score. 100% = full speed. At 70%, the phone is throttling, which is why your game stutters.

    How to use it for gaming
    • Once: while the phone is cool (not gaming, not charging), tap Calibrate. The baseline is saved on this phone.
    • After gaming: open ThermoCheck. The reading appears right away. Below 75%? Give the phone a break.
    • Browsers pause background tabs, so the site can't measure while a game is open. Check before and after playing, or between matches.
    • Tap Share → Add to Home Screen (iPhone) or ⋮ → Install app (Android) to open it like an app.
    Tips to reduce heat
    • Don't game while charging. It's the #1 cause of heat.
    • Take the case off for long sessions.
    • Lower graphics / FPS in the game settings (e.g. 60 → 40 FPS).
    • Lower brightness and close background apps.
    • Avoid direct sun, and don't play on a bed or pillow.
    • Use a clip-on phone cooler for long sessions.
